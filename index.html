Mission

Minutes matter.
Information first.

HARD should reach an emergency scene fast and give responders a live view before ground crews arrive.

The project focuses on a fast, stable FPV drone for emergency response and reconnaissance, developed and tested through simulation and physical prototypes.

Read the project brief

Current direction

A practical starting point.

01

High-speed FPV flight

The vehicle should be a fast, agile FPV drone that a pilot can fly quickly to a scene and hold steady once there.

02

FPGA integration

An FPGA should support onboard processing, such as helping the operator spot people and hazards in the live video.

03

Iterative testing

Simulation, bench testing, and flight testing should guide each design decision.
