Why R₀?

The zeroth response

Before the first response comes R₀.

R₀ takes its name from the idea of a zeroth response: a drone sent ahead to help first responders understand a scene before they arrive. The zero marks that early step—gathering a live view of conditions so the people responding can make better-informed decisions.

Our goal is to support first responders with earlier information, helping them prepare for what comes next.

Current direction

Project overview

High-speed FPV flight

The goal is a fast, agile FPV drone that could reach around 100 mph, then hold steady over a scene.

Stereo camera + FPGA

The FPGA should sync and timestamp stereo frames and IMU data, then send matched, numbered image pairs to a ground computer for SLAM.

Iterative testing

Simulation, bench tests, and flight trials should check speed, steady observation, and stereo data quality as the design develops.
